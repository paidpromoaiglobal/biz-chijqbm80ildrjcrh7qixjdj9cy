Our Story

Zeeshan Beg Center

Zeeshan Beg Center is a well-established clothing store located in the heart of Jorhat's Mozhar Market on A T Road, opposite BB Hall. We bring you a wide range of fashionable and affordable clothing options for men, women, and children, ensuring every customer walks out feeling confident and stylish. Our prime location makes us easily accessible to shoppers from across Jorhat and the surrounding areas of Assam.

At Zeeshan Beg Center, we believe that great fashion should be accessible to everyone. Our store is stocked with the latest trends as well as timeless classics, catering to diverse tastes and occasions – from everyday wear to festive and special occasion outfits. We are committed to providing a warm, welcoming shopping experience with personalized attention and quality products at competitive prices.

  • Conveniently located in Mozhar Market, A T Road, Jorhat – easily accessible from all parts of the city
  • Wide variety of clothing for men, women, and children under one roof
  • Affordable pricing with a focus on quality fabrics and latest fashion trends
